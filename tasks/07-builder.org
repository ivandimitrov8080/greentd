This file plans the builder hero that the client does not yet have at all: its
entity and position, XP and levels, skill points, the seven skills, its mana
pool, auto-blink and the Command Book.  It touches =src/sim.rs= (the new
=src/sim/builder.rs= module), =src/protocol.rs=, =src/server.rs=,
=src/client.rs=, =src/visuals.rs= and =assets/balance/builder.ron=.  Numbers
research could not confirm are marked =(tune)=.

* The builder entity

** TODO [#A] Introduce the builder as a player-owned hero entity                                                                 :@builder:
   :PROPERTIES:
   :ID:         builder-001
   :EFFORT:     1w
   :END:
   The client has no builder at all: a left-click is translated straight into
   =ClientCmd::Build { x, y, kind }= in =src/client.rs= with no unit and no
   position.  Give each player a builder unit with a position on the map and make
   the build command originate from it; that is also what gives range-limited
   building and auto-blink anything to measure.

   - Files: =src/sim/builder.rs=, =src/protocol.rs=, =src/visuals.rs=, =src/client.rs=
   - Acceptance:
     - [ ] Each player owns exactly one builder with a position on the map, replicated to every peer.
     - [ ] A =ClientCmd::Build= is validated against the builder's position and is no longer accepted unconditionally.
     - [ ] The builder is an authority-owned sim entity; a client cannot teleport it arbitrarily.
     - [ ] A player with no builder cannot build.

* Non-goals

  - Not the summonable tower entity itself ([[file:05-towers.org][05-towers.org]]
    =tower-016=); this file owns only the skill that summons it.
  - Not the economy numbers the bonuses feed
    ([[file:06-economy.org][06-economy.org]]): the skill contributes a delta, and the economy file owns
    the totals.
  - Not the crit system or the food cap
    ([[file:05-towers.org][05-towers.org]] =tower-018=, [[file:06-economy.org][06-economy.org]] =econ-007=); this
    file only supplies the skill contributions.
  - Not the lobby's difficulty and mode selection
    ([[file:08-lobby.org][08-lobby.org]]).

* Open questions

  - XP curve.  Recommended default: 10 xp per kill, 1000 xp (100 kills) per
    level, max level 30, all  =(tune)=.
  - Starting mana.  Recommended default: 20  =(tune)=.
  - Re-spec policy.  Recommended default: none — skills are permanent once
    spent.
  - Intelligence to mana.  Recommended default: max mana = 100 + 10 × int,
    regen = 1 + 0.1 × int per second, both  =(tune)=.
  - Gold Bonus coefficient.  Recommended default: 100, decreasing 10 per take,
    with a floor of 10  =(tune)=.
  - Auto-blink threshold.  Recommended default: 1200 units  =(tune)=.
