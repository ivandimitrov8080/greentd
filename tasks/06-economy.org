This file plans the per-player economy: the resource model that replaces
=Player { gold, kills, connected }=, end-of-wave gold, income, bounty, sell
values, food, lumber, gambling, transmute, the leaver split and the HUD data
contract.  It touches =src/sim.rs= (the =src/sim/economy.rs= and
=src/sim/rng.rs= modules introduced by [[file:02-simulation.org][02-simulation.org]] =sim-001=),
=src/game.rs=, =src/protocol.rs=, =src/server.rs=, =src/client.rs= and
=assets/balance/economy.ron=.  Numbers research could not confirm are marked
=(tune)=.

* Resource model

* Open questions

  - Exact end-of-wave gold curve (200 start, 10–20 step).  Recommended default:
    start 200, +15 per wave  =(tune)=.
  - Starting gold.  Recommended default: 500 (Evolution)  =(tune)=.
  - Whether income ticks on a timer, at wave end, or both.  Recommended default:
    at end of wave only, to keep it replayable.
  - Food cap.  Recommended default: 10  =(tune)=.
  - Whether lumber is on by default.  Recommended default: off for the faithful
    36-wave modes, on for the Evolution-style modes.
  - Jackpot base and payout maths.  Recommended default: base 30, payout
    = 2 × base + accumulated  =(tune)=.
  - Transmute conversion rate, and whether it may create gold from nothing.
    Recommended default: converts at the tower's current sell value and never
    creates gold outright.
  - "Every 10 waves 1k gold" and "final waves 300".  Recommended default:
    implement both as data toggles, marked  =(tune)=.
